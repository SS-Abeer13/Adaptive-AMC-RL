4. LATENCY ANALYSIS & KEY TAKEAWAYS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Strict 3GPP TTI Budget Compliance:
     In 5G NR (e.g. 15 kHz subcarrier spacing), the subframe/TTI duration is 1000 us (1 ms);
     at 30 kHz numerology, TTI is 500 us; at 60 kHz numerology, TTI is 250 us.
     - The vectorized / batched actor policy forward step on CPU takes:
       * ONNX Runtime: ~82 us (N=10) to ~240 us (N=100) median latency.
       * NumPy: ~155 us (N=10) to ~300 us (N=100) median latency.
       * MATLAB (full policy step including feature creation): ~400 us (N=10) to ~3 ms (N=100).
     - Both ONNX Runtime and NumPy inference comfortably fit within a 500 us or 1 ms TTI budget,
       confirming that neural AMC decision-making is readily deployable in real-time L2/MAC schedulers.
  2. Vectorized Batched vs Per-User Execution:
     Batching users across the neural network yields dramatic speedups over sequential per-user calls:
     at N=100, batched ONNX is ~19x faster (237 us vs 4.5 ms) and batched NumPy is ~18x faster (286 us vs 5.3 ms).
  3. Baseline Overhead Comparison:
     - Fixed AMC lookup is trivial (median 0.8 - 2.4 us in MATLAB).
     - Context-joint-500 multi-dimensional index lookup requires ~4 - 9 us in batched mode.
     - OLLA outer-loop step-size adjustment requires ~18 - 23 us in batched mode.
