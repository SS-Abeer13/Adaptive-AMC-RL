\section*{How to read this supplement}\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{How to read this supplement}
All files are also provided as CSV or Markdown in the accompanying archive, with SHA-256 hashes in \texttt{MANIFEST.sha256}. S1 is the case registry: 67 cases and 108 scheme evaluations of the main round (v9--v17), plus the ledger of the 40 cases and 74 scheme evaluations of the pre-registered robustness checks (107 cases, 182 scheme evaluations in total). S2 gives the per-case comparator tables that exist as result tables (v16, v17 and every robustness stage, including the one-step learner controls R1, R1b, their replication R1c and the full-action RL run R6); for v9--v15 the registry row carries the margin over the best rule, and the per-trace results are in the code and data release. S4 lists the screened learning-based link-adaptation papers with the codes behind the counts of Sections I and II. S3 lists every frozen pre-registration with its SHA-256 hash, the commit in which that hash entered \texttt{FREEZE.sha256}, the first runner start of the stage (runner heartbeat); all times are UTC+6, the time zone of the run PC and the commit that added its results; the full texts follow. S5 holds the configuration audit and the Deviation~D1 verification note. Deviation~D1: a symmetric configuration audit of all 13 stages (\texttt{Optimized/v17x/AUDIT\_config\_all\_stages.md}) found no other mismatch; a diagnostic cross-check that used 500 instead of 300 G2 traces explains an apparent discrepancy (\texttt{G2\_D1\_verification\_note.md}). S6 reproduces the diagnostic and sensitivity reports (end-of-trace accounting, PF-averaging definition, fresh-trace summary, symmetric seed check, decision time, headroom) verbatim. Evidence eligibility: a valid status alone does not make a case evidence. Of the 67 main-round cases, 58 are eligible as evidence; cases with a win: 9 with a valid status and corrected verdict, 8 eligible (S1, \texttt{case\_eligibility.csv}). Margins are relative differences in percent, paired per trace; ``beats'' follows the definition in Section V of the paper (Holm-adjusted Wilcoxon $p<0.05$ and 90\,\% CI lower bound above zero).
