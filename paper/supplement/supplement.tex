\section*{How to read this supplement}\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{How to read this supplement}
All files are also provided as CSV or Markdown in the accompanying archive, with SHA-256 hashes in \texttt{MANIFEST.sha256}. S1 is the case registry: 67 cases and 108 scheme evaluations of the main round (v9--v17), plus the ledger of the 9 cases and 18 scheme evaluations of the pre-registered robustness checks (76 cases, 126 scheme evaluations in total). S2 gives the per-case comparator tables that exist as result tables (v16, v17 and every robustness stage); for v9--v15 the registry row carries the margin over the best rule, and the per-trace results are in the code and data release. S3 lists every frozen pre-registration with its SHA-256 hash, the commit in which that hash entered \texttt{FREEZE.sha256}, the first runner start of the stage (runner heartbeat); all times are UTC+6, the time zone of the run PC and the commit that added its results; the full texts follow. Margins are relative differences in percent, paired per trace; ``beats follows rule R1 of the paper (Holm-adjusted Wilcoxon $p<0.05$ and 90\,\% CI lower bound above zero).
